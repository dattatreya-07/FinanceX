## 2. Detailed Course Blueprints

### Course 1: NISM Series VIII: Equity Derivatives Certification Masterclass
- **Learning Objective**: Master derivatives pricing, directional and volatility hedging strategies, portfolio margin calculation, and SEBI compliance guidelines.
- **Topic Alignment**:
  - `Topic 2`: Futures Contracts & Mechanics
  - `Topic 3`: Options Pricing & Trading Strategies
  - `Topic 6`: Risk Management & Regulatory Framework
- **Lessons**:
  1. `L1`: *Fundamentals of Futures Contracts & Cost of Carry Model* (Fair Value = $S_0 \cdot e^{(r-q)T}$, Arbitrage bounds, Contango vs Backwardation).
  2. `L2`: *Option Greeks Deep Dive: Delta, Gamma & Theta Decay* (Black-Scholes partial derivatives, Gamma risk in expiry week, Delta-neutral rebalancing).
  3. `L3`: *Multi-Leg Strategies: Bull Spreads, Straddles & Iron Condors* (Net debit/credit payoffs, break-even calculations, volatility smile trading).
  4. `L4`: *Regulatory Safeguards, Margining & SEBI Risk Framework* (SPAN + Exposure margin, Peak margin reporting, client-level position limits).

---

### Course 2: Fixed Income & Debt Securities Analysis
- **Learning Objective**: Analyze sovereign and corporate debt instruments, price complex coupon structures, calculate duration and convexity, and manage interest rate risk.
- **Topic Alignment**:
  - `Topic 4`: Fixed Income & Debt Instruments
- **Lessons**:
  5. `L5`: *Bond Pricing Mechanics, Spot Rates & Yield to Maturity (YTM)* (Discounted cash flow bond pricing, semi-annual compounding, clean vs dirty price).
  6. `L6`: *Macaulay Duration, Modified Duration & Convexity Hedging* (Price sensitivity $\Delta P/P \approx -D_{mod} \cdot \Delta y + \frac{1}{2} C (\Delta y)^2$).
  7. `L7`: *Term Structure of Interest Rates & Credit Default Spreads* (Expectations hypothesis, Liquidity preference, credit ratings & spread widening).

---

### Course 3: NISM Series XV: Research Analyst Certification
- **Learning Objective**: Conduct rigorous corporate equity research, evaluate income statements, balance sheets, and cash flows, build DCF valuation models, and adhere to SEBI research analyst governance.
- **Topic Alignment**:
  - `Topic 5`: Equity Research & Valuation
  - `Topic 6`: Risk Management & Regulatory Framework
- **Lessons**:
  8. `L8`: *Fundamental Analysis, Financial Statement Reconstruction & Free Cash Flow* (FCFF vs FCFE derivations, quality of earnings, working capital adjustments).
  9. `L9`: *Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Modeling & Relative Valuation Multiples* (WACC calculation, Terminal Value perpetuity vs Exit Multiple, EV/EBITDA, P/E PEG).
  10. `L10`: *SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 & Code of Conduct* (Conflict of interest disclosures, public appearance guidelines, trading restrictions).

---

### Course 4: Financial Fundamentals: The Evolution of Money & Markets
- **Learning Objective**: Understand the historical progression of currency systems, the mechanics of modern central banking, primary capital formation, secondary exchange order books, and asset allocation.
- **Topic Alignment**:
  - `Topic 1`: Financial Markets Architecture
- **Lessons**:
  11. `L11`: *From Barter to Digital Fiat: The Architecture of Money & Central Banking* (Functions of money, fractional reserve banking, repo/reverse repo transmission).
  12. `L12`: *Capital Formation, Primary Issues & Secondary Exchange Order Books* (IPO book-building, price discovery, continuous limit order books, bid-ask spreads).
  13. `L13`: *Risk-Return Profiles, Modern Portfolio Theory & Asset Allocation* (Efficient frontier, Sharpe ratio, systemic vs unsystemic risk diversification).

## 3. Question Bank & Assessment Taxonomy Blueprint

Questions are distributed across 5 Bloom cognitive levels:
1. **Knowledge (20%)**: Terminology, definitions, regulatory rules.
2. **Understanding (25%)**: Conceptual relationships (e.g. interest rate impact on bond prices).
3. **Application (30%)**: Numerical calculations (fair value, duration, option payoffs, DCF valuation).
4. **Analysis & Decision (25%)**: Scenario analysis, strategy selection under market conditions.

### Assessment Target Matrix
- **Total Authoritative Questions**: 24+ high-quality questions (spanning all 4 courses and 6 topics).
- **Quizzes**: 5 structured quizzes (Futures, Options, Fixed Income, Equity Research, Market Fundamentals).
- **Mock Exams**: 3 comprehensive mock exams with complete question mappings and realistic SEBI/NISM pass rules.
